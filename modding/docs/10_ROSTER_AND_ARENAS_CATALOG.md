# Shadow Fight 2 — Character & Arena Catalog

This catalog documents the combatants, bosses, bodyguards, and arenas available across the Shadow Fight 2 engine runtime.

---

## 1. Bosses & Bodyguards (Story Progression)

### Act 1: Hero Reborn (`ZONE_1`)
* **Boss**: **Lynx** (`lynx` / `BOSS_LYNX`) — Uses Lynx Claws with time bomb enchantment, smoke bomb invisibility.
* **Bodyguards**:
  1. `shin` — Knives
  2. `brick` — Steel Batons
  3. `needle` — Sai
  4. `ghost` — Ninja Sword
  5. `dagger` — Daggers
* **Primary Arena**: `arena` (Tournament Ring) / `bamboo_grove` / `mountain`

---

### Act 2: Secret Path (`ZONE_2`)
* **Boss**: **Hermit** (`hermit` / `BOSS_HERMIT`) — Uses Hermit's Swords, Lightning magic, Water ball, Levitation.
* **Bodyguards**:
  1. `dragon` — Polearm
  2. `buffalo` — Crescent Knives
  3. `mantis` — Krises
  4. `tiger` — Improvised Claws
  5. `crane` — Katana
* **Arenas**: `autumn`, `chess_yard`, `emerald_forest`, `night_bridge`, `sakura`, `stone_dragon`

---

### Act 3: Trail of Blood (`ZONE_3`)
* **Boss**: **Butcher** (`butcher` / `BOSS_BUTCHER`) — Uses Meat Cleavers, Earthquake shockwave stomp.
* **Bodyguards**:
  1. `bird` — Knuckles
  2. `rhino` — Spiked Clubs
  3. `bull` — Maces
  4. `viper` — Kusarigama
  5. `raven` — Heavy Sword
* **Arenas**: `castle_and_bridge`, `lamps_on_water`, `pink_lake`, `swamp`, `village`

---

### Act 4: Pirate Throne (`ZONE_4`)
* **Boss**: **Wasp** (`wasp` / `BOSS_WASP`) — Uses Naginata, Wall-bounce flying strike.
* **Bodyguards**:
  1. `kraken` — Claws
  2. `cleaver` — Dual axes
  3. `shark` — Harpoon
  4. `hawk` — Staves
  5. `whale` — Anchor / Heavy Mace
* **Arenas**: `ships`, `cave`, `flowers_field`, `dark_room`, `heaven`

---

### Act 5: Great Temptation (`ZONE_5`)
* **Boss**: **Widow** (`widow` / `BOSS_WIDOW`) — Uses Steel Fans, Ice teleportation magic, Life steal.
* **Bodyguards**:
  1. `irida` — Battle Hammers
  2. `fox` — Daggers
  3. `cleo` — Scythe
  4. `puma` — Tonfas
  5. `mistress` — Magari Yari
* **Arenas**: `fuji`, `snowy_peak`, `volcano`, `battlefield`, `flooded_village`, `ruins_village`

---

### Act 6: Iron Reign (`ZONE_6`)
* **Boss**: **Shogun** (`shogun` / `BOSS_SHOGUN`) — Uses Daisho (Katana + Wakizashi), summons ninja bodyguards mid-fight.
* **Bodyguards**:
  1. `corporal` — Yari
  2. `captain` — Dual Swords
  3. `major` — Nunchaku
  4. `colonel` — Dadao
  5. `general` — Silver Glaive
* **Arenas**: `shadow_gate`, `burning_town`, `flying_rocks`, `ice_cave`, `waterfall`, `graveyard_ships`, `magic_rocks`

---

### Act 7: Revelation / Gates of Shadows (`ZONE_7`)
* **Boss**: **Titan** (`titan` / `BOSS_TITAN`) — Giant mech warlord with Desolator heavy blade, torso grapple claw, and kinetic shield.
* **Bodyguards**:
  1. `assassin`
  2. `master`
  3. `guru`
  4. `emperor`
  5. `corsair`
* **Arenas**: `spaceship`, `neural_network`, `factory`, `capsules`, `stone_forest`, `eggs`, `skyport`

---

## 2. Tournament & Special Sparring Fighters

* **`punchbag`** — Training Punching Bag (immobile, infinite health, ideal for combo training).
* **`monkey`** — Stage 1 Tournament fighter (unarmed bare-hands punches and kicks).
* **`velvet`** — Stage 2 Tournament fighter (Knives).
